## Episode 1

From a Wayback Machine capture of the watch page, looked up by the owner:

- Title "Jake and Amir Present: Lonely and Horny Episode 1", on the CollegeHumor
  channel, published 2016-04-12, 892,619 views at capture.
- Description: "Jake Hurwitz and Amir Blumenfeld are back! In their first show
  since "Jake and Amir," the guys explore love and dating in 2016. Watch all ten
  episodes, only on Vimeo on Demand!"

So the show began on Vimeo on Demand in 2016, ten episodes, with episode 1 free
on YouTube, two years before Dropout launched. Entries 2–5 were added in a
weekly run from December 2018, Dropout's launch season, and are still to be
identified. Wayback lookups for each:
`https://web.archive.org/web/*/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<id>*`
(archive.org is unreachable from the cloud sessions this project runs in).
