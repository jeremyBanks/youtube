# Quarantine boundaries to check by eye

A worksheet for a person to settle where quarantine production starts and stops
on each show. It goes with `quarantine-episodes.md`, which has the evidence.
Each table is the stretch either side of a suspected boundary, in release order.
Tick ☐ → ☑ for a quarantine episode, and leave a note where it is unclear.
Nothing in the catalogue has been marked.

What to look for: separate boxes or home backgrounds, call lag or webcam
quality, Roll20 or a virtual table instead of the Dome's dice and minis, or a
host and guest never in the same shot. An in-studio shoot under distancing
rules, such as Misfits and Magic, with one camera per person and nobody in the
same shot, does **not** count.

One person alone in the Dome because they are isolating, such as Brennan in Mice
and Murder or in "A Safe Return to the Dome", **does** count.

Order and dates are the curation's `published:`, our best understanding of each
video's original release. Where Dropout's own release date differs it is shown
beside it, as a prompt to check the curated date, not a correction. Release can
trail production by months, so a date near the line proves nothing on its own.
Links: **members** is the @Dropout members upload, **free** the public one,
**Dropout** the watch.dropout.tv page.
