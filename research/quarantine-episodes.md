# Quarantine episodes

**Quarantine episode** means content made under COVID distancing: the cast at home, on a video call, using Roll20 or backdrops
instead of a shared set. In-studio shoots with distancing rules, such as Misfits and Magic (one camera per person, nobody in the
same shot), do **not** count. They are listed as negatives.

This is research only. Second pass, 2026-10-20. Nothing in `curation/`, `config/`, `src/` or `data/` was changed.

Dates: **curated** means `published:` in `curation/seasons.yaml`, which is usually the YouTube date. **Dropout** means
`releaseDate` in `data/dropout.yaml`. Where they differ, both are given. Several curated episode 1s carry a late YouTube date
(for example, Pirates ep 1 is 2021-11-16), so stretches are given by Dropout release order.

Confidence:
- **confirmed**: a sourced statement covers the whole stretch.
- **likely**: strong inference, or a fan wiki without a primary citation.
- **unknown**: we looked and could not settle it.

## Where the studio returned

- **Dimension 20 / Dropout overall.** Misfits and Magic "was recorded in late March 2021" and is "the first season to be recorded
  in-person in the dome since *Fearful Symmetry*". It was announced in "Dimension 20 Update: A Safe Return to the Dome", whose
  Dropout description is "Brennan announces a return to the dome and details the safety protocols we will be adhering to". Wikipedia
  dates it 2021-05-24. The Seven was filmed May 2021 and Shriek Week the week of 2021-07-18, both in the Dome. A Starstruck Odyssey
  (fall 2021) was the first Intrepid Heroes season back. Coffin Run (2022) was the first with physical sets and minis again.
  Sources: https://dimension20.fandom.com/api.php?action=parse&page=Misfits_and_Magic&prop=wikitext&format=json (and the same with
  `page=The_Seven`, `Shriek_Week`, `A_Starstruck_Odyssey` and `Coffin_Run`); https://watch.dropout.tv/videos/dimension-20-update-a-safe-return-to-the-dome
- **Game Changer.** Season 4 was in studio. In `404. Secret Samta 3` (2021-12-13), Carolyn Page is introduced as "Making her
  in-person debut, it's virtual champion Carolyn Page!" Source: https://gamechanger.fandom.com/wiki/Secret_Samta_3 (fetched through
  api.php). Wikipedia also says Season 4 returned to the studio. Curated S4 begins with `403. Noise Boys (Make Some Noise 4)`
  (2021-11-20). Episodes 401 and 402 carry later curated dates.
- **Um, Actually.** "Upon the show's return to the studio in Season 6...". Studio episodes begin with `601. Um Actually Smackdown`
  (2022-01-14). Source: https://um-actually.fandom.com/wiki/Michael_Saltzman and `/wiki/Fact_Checker`.

The best company-wide figure is that studio production resumed between **late March 2021** (the Misfits shoot) and **2021-05-24**
(the announcement). Release dates trail production by months, so remote Um, Actually episodes kept airing until 2021-12-31.

## Dimension 20, one section per campaign

### Fantasy High (Chapter 2) — Sophomore Year: **finale is quarantine**, the rest is in studio
- The season was streamed live on Twitch from the Dome, as "Dimension 20 LIVE", from Oct 2019. That was a web stream with no
  in-person audience, and it was **not** quarantine.
- The penultimate episode, planned for 2020-03-18, was postponed "due to health concerns surrounding COVID-19". The finale was
  "moved out of the Dome and onto a virtual platform". It was pre-recorded, ran 8 hours, and was split into the two curated
  episodes `19./20. Spring Break! I Believe In You! (Part 1/2)` (2020-04-05 / 04-07). The last episode filmed in the Dome was
  `18. Fearful Symmetry` (2020-03-16).
- Sources: D20 wiki pages "Fantasy High: Sophomore Year Episodes" and "Fireside Chats with Brennan & Friends" (read through
  `https://dimension20.fandom.com/api.php?action=parse&page=...`); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimension_20 . **Confirmed.**
- `College Visit` (RTX @ Home, Sept 2020) and `Boys' Night` (Roll20Con, 2020-10-24) are both "Location: Remote" on the wiki's
  "Live Shows" page. **Confirmed.** The curated titles match by name and date.

### A Crown of Candy — **in studio** (confirmed)
- "The season was filmed in August 2019" (D20 wiki). Episodes `1. There Is Strength in Sweetness` 2020-04-08 → `17. For Candia! (Part 2)`
  aired under lockdown but are not quarantine episodes.
- Its Adventuring Party talkbacks (S1, `bts:` 2020-04-12 → 2020-08-26) **are**: they were livestreamed remotely. Local blurb for
  `I Stan The Protestant Work Ethic!`: "zoom call shark fins".

### Pirates of Leviathan — **quarantine** (confirmed)
- "This season was filmed the week of July 20, 2020 ... the first season of Dimension 20 to have [been remotely recorded] ...
  takes place in the 'theater of the mind'" (D20 wiki, page `Pirates_of_Leviathan`). TheGamer interview with Brennan Lee Mulligan,
  2020-10-09: https://www.thegamer.com/brennan-lee-mulligan-dnd-dimension-20-covid-interview/
- Stretch: everything curated, from the trailer (2020-08-12) through ep 6 (2020-10-20), plus AP S2 `Look At All My Friends!` and
  `The Buccaneer Buddies Reunite`. Ep 1 carries a 2021-11-16 YouTube date. The animation (2022) is outside the stretch.

### The Unsleeping City (Chapter 2) — **quarantine** (confirmed)
- Our own YouTube trailer description: "Remotely-shot to keep everyone in the cast and production team as safe as possible".
  The D20 wiki says it was "filmed remotely", "the first to feature battle maps using Roll20 instead of physical maps and minis",
  and "filmed in the midst of lock down".
- Stretch: trailer 2020-10-30 → ep 18 (2021-02-20), plus AP S3 `A Ferry Special Episode` 2020-11-17 → `Crew Edition` 2021-03-22.
  Excluded: `Gauntlet at the Garden Trailer` (2024) and the animations.
- AP S3 `I Really Miss Smelling My Friends` answers a fan question about "playing this season remote".

### Mice and Murder — **quarantine, hybrid** (confirmed)
- "Filmed in four back-to-back days ... in October 2020". "DM Brennan Lee Mulligan filmed in the Dome, while the rest of the players
  filmed at home with physical colored and backlit backdrops, not greenscreens, like The Unsleeping City: Chapter II". Roll20 was
  used, and minis were reshot afterwards. These quotes are from the D20 wiki, citing Dropout Discord posts by Erin Hassidim and
  Grant O'Brien, and the Mice & Murder FAQ.
- This also settles the green-screen question: the backdrops were physical, and **no green screen** was used for UC2 or Mice and Murder.
- Stretch: trailer 2021-03-24 → `10. Unfinished Business` (Dropout 2021-06-09), plus AP S4 `Say Hi, Sylvan Sleuths!` 2021-04-07 →
  `Behind-the-Screams (Crew Edition)` 2021-06-11. AP S4 was "pre-recorded in batches", and the wiki gives no setup. Wikipedia
  counts AP S1–4 as remote.

### Misfits and Magic (Chapter 1), The Seven, Shriek Week, A Starstruck Odyssey — **in studio** (confirmed)
- Misfits: recorded late March 2021 in the Dome, with one camera per player and no group shots. The Seven: May 2021, same setup.
  Shriek Week: week of 2021-07-18, four players in the Dome, group shots back. Starstruck: fall 2021, in the Dome.
- **Their Adventuring Party talkbacks (S5–S7) are likely quarantine.** The wiki marks AP S8 ep 1 `Our Tail Was Not Between Our Legs`
  (Dropout 2022-01-13) as "the first in-person Adventuring Party" and "the first episode of Dimension 20 back in The Dome during
  the Covid-19 pandemic". It does not say how S5–S7 were made. Stretch if so: `Cluck If You Buck` 2021-07-01 →
  `Oh No, I'm In Love With Chupacabra` 2021-12-08. **Likely.**
- Note that "first back in The Dome" there contradicts the Misfits page. It probably means "first without distancing", but this is
  unresolved.

### Tiny Heist, Escape from the Bloodkeep — **in studio** (confirmed)
- Tiny Heist was "filmed over two Wednesdays in April 2019". Bloodkeep was filmed 2019-02-16/17 (D20 wiki).

### Later remote talkbacks, not quarantine
- AP S10 `Do Clothes Die?` ("join virtually") and `Schrödinger's Canon`, and AP S11 `Gatorade Shampoo` ("Zoom energy"), 2022.
  These are remote, but they are outside the COVID stretch and not distancing-driven as far as we know. They are recorded here so
  that a decision on the marker can cover them.

## Game Changer — Season 3 is quarantine (confirmed)
- Wikipedia: "recorded via video conference to comply with lockdown restrictions". It cites a Dropout tweet of 2020-07-07:
  "Shooting remotely opens up new possibilities of chaos...". The Game Changer wiki states "This episode was recorded remotely" on
  every S3 episode page, and its Season 3 page says "The entire season was filmed remotely."
- Stretch: `301. Tell Us About Yourself` 2020-09-15 → `315. Secret Samta 2: The Samta Clause` 2021-04-16 (Dropout 2021-04-16).
- Season 2 (`202. Do I Hear $1?` 2020-03-06 → `206. Yes or No` 2020-05-08) aired during lockdown. It is presumed filmed in studio
  before then, but no source was found, so this is likely rather than confirmed.
- Season 4 is in studio (see above).
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_Changer_(game_show) ; https://gamechanger.fandom.com/wiki/Season_3

  lockdown. The rest have Dropout dates from April to June 2020. `309. A Report From the CollegeHumor Office` (2020-04-20) is set in
  the office. No source was found for whether any S3 episode was made at home.
- **Seasons 4–5: unknown.** Season 4 runs 2021-04-28 → 2021-07-21; Season 5 runs 2021-08-20 → 2022-03-17. We searched the web for

  hit for remote, COVID or quarantine). Nothing settles it. S4 began after the studio return, so S4 shot in studio is plausible

## Method

- Local: a regex for zoom, remote, from home, quarantine, video chat, socially distanced, virtual, pandemic and covid, run over
  `title` and `description` of `data/dropout.yaml` and `data/videos/*.yaml`. A miss is not evidence of studio production, because
  most blurbs never mention the setup.
- Local: curated releases from 2020-03 to 2022-06 per show and season, plus Dropout `releaseDate` per collection.
- Fan wikis, read through the MediaWiki API (`api.php?action=parse&prop=wikitext`) and full-text search (`list=search`) for
  remote, remotely, quarantine and COVID: dimension20.fandom.com, gamechanger.fandom.com, um-actually.fandom.com and
  dropout.fandom.com. A plain WebFetch of the D20 wiki returns HTTP 402, but the API works. The Wayback Machine was not needed.
- Web: Wikipedia (Dimension 20, Game Changer, Um, Actually, Dropout), TheGamer, geekpeek, Bossrush, and searches for Sam Reich
  interviews, Breaking News, Erotic Book Club and Adventuring Academy. None of those searches found production detail.
- Fan-wiki claims are only as good as their citations. The D20 ones cite Dropout Discord staff posts and the Mice & Murder FAQ;
  the Game Changer and Um Actually ones cite nothing.
